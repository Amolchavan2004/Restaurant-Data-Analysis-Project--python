
# 🍽️ Restaurant Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Title  
**Restaurant Price, Rating & Discount Analysis**  
**Author: Amol Chavan **

---

## 📊 Project Overview  

This project focuses on analyzing restaurant data collected through web scraping from **EazyDiner**. The main goal is to understand pricing trends, customer ratings, discount patterns, and city-wise restaurant behavior to extract meaningful business insights. 

---

## 🎯 Objectives  

- Analyze city-wise restaurant prices, ratings, and discounts  
- Identify value-for-money restaurants  
- Study the relationship between price and customer ratings  
- Understand discount distribution patterns  
- Compare restaurant trends across different cities 

---

## 🌐 Data Source  

- Website Used: **EazyDiner**  
- Data Collection Method: **Web Scraping**

---

## 📁 Dataset Features (Columns)  

The dataset contains the following important columns:

- Restaurant Name  
- Rating  
- Cuisine Served  
- Location (City)  
- Approximate Cost for Two  
- Discount Percentage 

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis  

### ✅ Univariate Analysis  

#### ⭐ Rating Distribution  
- Most restaurants have ratings between **4.0 and 4.5**  
- Indicates generally high customer satisfaction  

#### 💸 Discount Distribution  
- Most common discounts range between **30% and 35%**  
- Larger discounts (40%–55%) exist but are less frequent  

#### 💰 Price Distribution  
- Majority of restaurants fall in the **₹1000 – ₹2000** price range  

#### 🍛 Most Common Cuisines  
- **Multicuisine** is the most popular  
- Followed by **North Indian** and **Indian**  
- Italian, European, and Café cuisines are less common   

---

### ✅ Bivariate Analysis  

#### 📈 Price vs Rating  
- No strong relationship observed  
- Restaurants across all price ranges mostly maintain ratings between **4.0 – 5.0**  

#### 🎯 Discount vs Rating  
- Discounts do not significantly impact restaurant ratings  

#### 🌍 City-wise Price Comparison  
- **Ahmedabad** has the lowest average restaurant prices  
- **Bengaluru** shows the highest price range  

#### 🏷️ City-wise Discount Comparison  
- **Pune** offers the highest overall discounts  

---

## 🏆 Key Insights  

- High ratings are consistent across price categories  
- Mid-range restaurants dominate the market  
- Discounts are more marketing-driven than quality-driven  
- Punjab Grill has the highest number of branches across cities  
- Pricing strategies vary significantly city-wise
